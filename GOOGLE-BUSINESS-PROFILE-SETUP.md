## ✅ **STEP 2: VERIFY YOUR BUSINESS**

**Google offers 3 verification methods:**

### **Method 1: Postcard (Most Common)**
- Google mails a postcard to your address
- Takes 5-7 days to arrive
- Postcard has 5-digit code
- Enter code in GBP dashboard

**How to request:**
1. In GBP dashboard, click "Verify now"
2. Select "Postcard"
3. Confirm address is correct
4. Click "Send postcard"
5. Wait 5-7 days
6. Check your mailbox daily
7. Postcard arrives → Enter code → Verified! ✅

---

### **Method 2: Phone Call (Instant)**
- Google calls your business phone
- Automated voice reads 6-digit code
- Enter code → Verified instantly!

**How to request:**
1. Click "Verify now"

3. Confirm number: +91 96760 77142
4. Click "Call now"

**Note:** Phone verification not available for all businesses. If you don't see this option, use postcard.

## 🚨 **COMMON ISSUES & FIXES**

### **Issue 1: "Verification postcard didn't arrive"**
**Wait:** 5-7 days  
**Check:** Spam/junk mail  
**Fix:** Request new postcard (GBP dashboard → "Didn't receive postcard?")

---

### **Issue 2: "Can't upload photos"**
**Check:** File size <5 MB  
**Check:** Format = JPG or PNG (not WebP)  
**Fix:** Resize images: https://www.iloveimg.com/resize-image

---

### **Issue 3: "Business keeps getting suspended"**
**Reasons:**
- Keyword stuffing in business name (don't add "luxury villas" to name)
- Wrong category selected
- Address not verifiable

**Fix:**
- Use exact legal business name only
- Verify with postcard (most reliable)
- Match address to official documents

---

### **Issue 4: "Competitors showing above me"**
**Why:**
- They have more reviews
- They have more photos
- They post more frequently

- Get 25+ reviews (use REVIEW-REQUEST-TEMPLATES.md)
- Upload 50+ photos
- Post 2-3X per week
- Complete 100% of profile
