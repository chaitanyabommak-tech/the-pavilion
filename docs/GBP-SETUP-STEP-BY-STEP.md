## 🚨 **Common Mistakes to Avoid**

❌ **Don't:**
- Use ALL CAPS in business name
- Add keywords to business name ("Best Villas Boduppal" is spam)
- Fake reviews from friends/employees
- Post promotional content only (mix in value)
- Ignore questions/reviews
- Use low-quality photos
- Duplicate profiles

✅ **Do:**
- Use actual business name
- Respond to all reviews (positive AND negative)
- Post consistently (1-2x per week)
- Upload high-quality photos
- Fill out every section completely
- Update hours for holidays
- Monitor insights monthly
