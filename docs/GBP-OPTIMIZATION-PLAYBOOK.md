## 9. Map Pack Ranking Factors {#ranking-factors}

Google's local ranking algorithm (simplified):

### Factor 1: Relevance (30%)
**Definition:** How well your GBP matches the search query.

**Optimization:**
- Primary category = `Real Estate Developer` (matches "villa developer" searches)
- Business name includes "The Pavillion" + "Bommaku Group" (matches brand searches)
- Description includes target keywords: luxury villa, Boduppal, standalone villas

### Factor 2: Distance (25%)
**Definition:** How close your business is to the searcher's location OR the location in their query.

**Optimization:**
- You can't change your physical location.
- **But:** Ensure your address PIN code (500039) is correct.
- **Service Areas:** Add service areas in GBP (Uppal, Ghatkesar, ECIL, Nagole, LB Nagar) — this helps ranking for "villas near Uppal" searches.

**Action:** Go to GBP → Info → Add Service Areas → Add: Uppal, Boduppal, Ghatkesar, Pocharam, ECIL, Nagole.

### Factor 3: Prominence (45%)
**Definition:** How well-known/popular your business is (online + offline).

**Signals Google Uses:**
1. **Number of reviews** (15%)
2. **Average star rating** (10%)
3. **Website authority** (backlinks, traffic) (10%)
4. **Citations (NAP mentions)** (5%)
5. **Social signals** (Facebook, Instagram engagement) (3%)
6. **User engagement with GBP** (clicks, calls, direction requests) (2%)

**Optimization Priority:**
1. Get to 50+ reviews (first 6 months)
2. Build website backlinks (local publications, real estate directories)
3. Submit NAP to top 20 local directories (Justdial, Sulekha, 99acres, MagicBricks, Housing.com)
4. Post regularly on social media, link to GBP
5. Drive GBP engagement: "Search for 'The Pavillion Boduppal' on Google and click our listing"

## 14. Red Flags & Suspension Risks

1. Stuff keywords in business name ("The Pavillion | Best Luxury Villas Boduppal | HMDA Approved") ❌
2. Create fake reviews (or pay for reviews) ❌
3. Use a virtual office/PO Box as address (must be physical location) ❌
4. Change business category frequently (looks spammy) ❌
