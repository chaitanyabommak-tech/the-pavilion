## 🚨 **COMMON ISSUES**

### **Issue 1: "Email not verified"**
- Check spam folder for verification email
- Click verification link
- Listing won't go live until verified

### **Issue 2: "Photo upload failed"**
- Reduce image size to <5 MB each
- Use JPG format (not PNG or WebP)
- Resize: https://www.iloveimg.com/resize-image

### **Issue 3: "Duplicate listing found"**
- Someone already listed your business
- Click "Claim this business"
- Verify ownership (phone/email OTP)

### **Issue 4: "Need GST number"**
- Most directories: GST is optional
- Skip that field
- If mandatory: Enter your GST or mark "N/A"
