## 🎨 **OPTION A: DIY ON CANVA (FREE, 1-2 HOURS)**

**Best for:** Budget-conscious, willing to spend 1-2 hours

### **Step-by-Step Canva Method:**

**Step 1: Sign Up on Canva**
- Go to: https://www.canva.com
- Click "Sign up with Google"
- Use: bommakugroup@gmail.com
- It's FREE!

**Step 2: Find Real Estate Catalog Template**
- Click "Templates" in left sidebar
- Search: "Real Estate Catalog" or "Property Brochure"
- Filter: "Free" (or pay ₹400/month for Pro templates)
- Pick a PREMIUM-looking template (elegant, minimalist)

**Step 3: Customize Cover Page**
- Click template to edit
- Change title to: "Villa Design Lookbook"
- Change subtitle: "50+ Exclusive Elevation & Interior Ideas"
- Upload The Pavillion logo (top or bottom corner)
- Replace placeholder image with your best villa photo

**Step 4: Create Introduction Page**
- Add new page (click "+" icon)
- Text block template or blank page
- Write:
```
Welcome to The Pavillion Villa Design Lookbook

This exclusive collection features 50+ premium villa designs curated just for you. Explore modern elevations, floor plan variations, and interior themes to inspire your dream home.

Every villa at The Pavillion is standalone with zero shared walls, giving you complete freedom to customize your design.

Use this lookbook to:
✓ Visualize your villa's exterior style
✓ Explore floor plan options
✓ Discover interior design themes
✓ Plan your customizations

Let's build your dream villa together!

Contact us:
📞 +91 96760 77142
🌐 bommakugroup.com
📧 bommakugroup@gmail.com
```

**Step 5: Add Elevation Design Pages (10 pages)**

**Where to get villa elevation images?**

**Source 1: Free Stock Photos**
- https://unsplash.com/s/photos/modern-villa
- https://pixabay.com/images/search/luxury-villa/
- https://pexels.com/search/villa-exterior/
- Search terms: "modern villa facade", "luxury villa exterior", "contemporary house elevation"
- Download 10 different styles (Modern, Traditional, Colonial, etc.)

**Source 2: AI-Generated (Free!)**
- https://www.bing.com/images/create (Microsoft Bing Image Creator)
- Prompts to use:
  ```
  "Modern luxury villa elevation, white facade, large windows, minimalist design, photorealistic"
  "Traditional Indian villa exterior, warm colors, arches, detailed architecture"
  "Contemporary villa facade, grey and wood, geometric design, beautiful landscaping"
  ```
- Generate 10 variations with different prompts
- Download all

**Source 3: Hire Architect Renderings (If Available)**
- Do you have 3D renderings from your architect?
- Use those (BEST option - shows actual project!)

**In Canva:**
- Add new page for each elevation
- Full-page image (no margins)
- Add text overlay at bottom:
  - Style name: "Modern Minimalist Villa"
  - Brief description: "Clean lines, large glass windows, flat roof, monochrome palette"
- Repeat for all 10 styles

**Step 6: Add Floor Plan Pages (3 pages)**

**Use Your Actual Floor Plans!**
- Go to your website: https://bommakugroup.com/the-pavillion
- Right-click floor plan images → "Save image as"
- Upload to Canva
- Create 3 pages:
  - Page 1: Ground floor (2 layout options side-by-side)
  - Page 2: First floor (2 layout options)
  - Page 3: Penthouse (2 layout options)

**Add labels:**
- "Ground Floor - Layout Option A" vs "Layout Option B"
- Highlight key features with arrows/callouts:
  - "Master bedroom with attached bath"
  - "Pooja room separate entrance"
  - "Open kitchen concept"

**Step 7: Add Interior Design Pages (3 pages)**

**Where to get interior photos?**
- https://unsplash.com/s/photos/luxury-living-room
- https://pixabay.com/images/search/modern-bedroom/
- https://pexels.com/search/villa-interior/

**3 Themes Per Room:**
- Living Room: Modern, Traditional, Contemporary
- Master Bedroom: Minimalist, Luxury, Cozy
- Kitchen: Modular, Open-concept, Traditional

**In Canva:**
- Page 1: Living Room (3 styles in grid layout)
- Page 2: Master Bedroom (3 styles)
- Page 3: Kitchen & Dining (3 styles)
- Add style names below each image

**Step 8: Add Customization Options Page**

**Create a table:**
```
What You Can Customize at The Pavillion

Elevation Style
• Modern, Traditional, Contemporary, Colonial, Mediterranean
• Facade materials: Stone, wood, glass, paint

Floor Plan Layout
• Bedroom positions
• Kitchen: Open vs closed
• Pooja room location
• Study room addition

Interiors
• Flooring: Tiles, marble, wood
• Wall finishes: Paint, wallpaper, texture
• Kitchen: Modular designs
• Bathroom: Fixtures and fittings

Landscaping
• Front yard design
• Backyard layout
• Driveway style
• Garden theme

100% CUSTOMIZATION FREEDOM
Your villa, your way!
```

**Step 9: Create Back Cover**

**Design:**
- Background: Elegant color (navy blue, dark green, or black)
- Center text:
```
Ready to Build Your Dream Villa?

Contact The Pavillion by Bommaku Group

📞 +91 96760 77142
🌐 bommakugroup.com
📧 bommakugroup@gmail.com
📍 Surya Hills, Boduppal, Hyderabad 500039

Visit us: Mon-Sat 10 AM - 6 PM, Sun 10 AM - 5 PM

Schedule your site visit today!
```

**Optional:** Add QR code
- Generate QR code: https://www.qr-code-generator.com/
- Link to: https://bommakugroup.com
- Download QR code image
- Upload to back cover (bottom right corner)

**Step 10: Download PDF**

- Click "Share" button (top right)
- Select "Download"
- Format: **PDF - Print** (best quality)
- Click "Download"
- Save as: `Villa-Design-Lookbook-The-Pavillion.pdf`
- Check file size: Should be <10 MB (for WhatsApp)

**If file size >10 MB:**
- Use online compressor: https://www.ilovepdf.com/compress_pdf
- Upload your PDF
- Download compressed version
- New size should be 5-8 MB

✅ **DONE! You have your lookbook!**

## 💼 **OPTION B: HIRE ON FIVERR (₹500-1500, 2-3 DAYS)**

**Best for:** Save time, get professional result

### **Step-by-Step Fiverr Method:**

**Step 1: Go to Fiverr**
- URL: https://www.fiverr.com
- Sign up (use: bommakugroup@gmail.com)

**Step 2: Search for Designers**
- Search: "real estate brochure design" or "property catalog design"
- Filter: Price ₹500-₹1500 (affordable range)
- Filter: Seller level "Level 1" or higher
- Filter: Delivery time "3 days or less"

**Step 3: Pick a Designer**
- Look for:
  - ✅ 4.8+ star rating
  - ✅ 50+ reviews
  - ✅ Portfolio shows real estate work
  - ✅ Fast response time
  - ✅ Offers revisions (1-2 revisions included)

**Step 4: Contact Designer**
- Click "Contact Seller"
- Message template:
```
Hi! I need a 15-20 page villa design lookbook PDF for my real estate project.

Requirements:
- Cover page with our logo
- 10 pages: Villa elevation designs (I'll provide photos or you source from stock)
- 3 pages: Floor plans (I'll provide)
- 3 pages: Interior design ideas (you source from stock)
- 1 page: Customization options table
- 1 page: Back cover with contact info
- Format: PDF, optimized for WhatsApp (<10 MB)
- Style: Premium, elegant, minimalist

Budget: ₹1,000-₹1,500
Deadline: 3 days

Can you do this? Please share similar samples from your portfolio.

Thanks!
```

**Step 5: Place Order**
- Designer responds with price quote (₹800-₹1,500 typical)
- If satisfied, click "Continue to Order"
- Select package (Basic or Standard)
- Pay via UPI, Card, or Net Banking

**Step 6: Provide Content**

**Send Designer:**

**Logos & Branding:**
- The Pavillion logo (if you have)
- Brand colors (if any)

**Text Content:**
- Cover page title: "Villa Design Lookbook"
- Introduction text (copy from Canva method Step 4)
- Contact info for back cover

**Images:**
- Your villa photos (if available)
- Floor plans (download from website, send as JPG)
- If you don't have villa photos: "Please source premium villa images from stock sites"

**Style Reference:**
- Link to 2-3 lookbooks you like (Google: "luxury villa brochure PDF")
- Example: "I like this style - minimalist, elegant, lots of white space"

**Step 7: Review & Revisions**
- Designer sends first draft (2-3 days)
- Review carefully
- Request changes:
  - "Change cover font to something more elegant"
  - "Replace page 5 image with this one"
  - "Add more spacing on page 8"
- Most gigs include 1-2 revisions FREE

**Step 8: Final Delivery**
- Approve final version
- Download PDF
- Check file size (<10 MB for WhatsApp)
- If >10 MB, ask designer to compress or do it yourself: https://www.ilovepdf.com/compress_pdf

**Step 9: Mark Complete**
- Mark order as complete on Fiverr
- Leave 5-star review (helps designer)

✅ **DONE! Professional lookbook ready!**

## ✅ **QUALITY CHECKLIST**

**Before finalizing your lookbook, check:**

**Design:**
- [ ] Cover looks premium (not cheap/cluttered)
- [ ] Consistent fonts throughout (max 2-3 font families)
- [ ] Color scheme matches brand (if applicable)
- [ ] High-resolution images (no blurry photos)
- [ ] Enough white space (not cramped)
- [ ] Professional layout (aligned, balanced)

**Content:**
- [ ] All text error-free (no typos!)
- [ ] Contact info correct (phone, email, website)
- [ ] 15-20 pages total
- [ ] Each page serves a purpose (no filler)
- [ ] Elevations show variety (10 different styles)
- [ ] Floor plans are clear and labeled

**Technical:**
- [ ] Format: PDF
- [ ] File size: <10 MB (for WhatsApp)
- [ ] Pages: 15-20
- [ ] Orientation: Portrait (vertical)
- [ ] Resolution: 300 DPI (print quality)
- [ ] No watermarks (looks unprofessional)

**Test:**
- [ ] Open on phone (readable?)
- [ ] Send to yourself on WhatsApp (works?)
- [ ] Ask friend: "Does this look premium?" (honest feedback)

✓ Use thick fonts (not thin/light fonts)
