4. Copy the short link (looks like: `https://g.page/r/...`)

### **Method 2: Manual Creation**
1. Find your Google Business Profile Place ID
2. Use this format:
   ```
   https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=YOUR_PLACE_ID
   ```

### **Method 3: Use Google Maps**
1. Search "The Pavillion Boduppal" on Google Maps
2. Click "Write a review"
3. Copy the URL from browser address bar

**⚠️ IMPORTANT:** Test the link yourself first! Make sure it opens directly to the review form.

## ❌ **WHAT NOT TO DO**

1. **Don't offer cash/discounts for reviews** (violates Google policies)
2. **Don't ask for only 5-star reviews** (looks fake, Google may remove)
3. **Don't write fake reviews yourself** (Google detects IP, device, pattern)
4. **Don't spam the same person >5 times** (annoys them, backfires)
5. **Don't ignore negative reviews** (respond professionally, show you care)
6. **Don't ask people who haven't visited** (low-quality reviews get flagged)
7. **Don't link reviews to booking** ("Review us and we'll give you a discount") — this violates Google's policies
